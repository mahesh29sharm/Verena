# Design notes

## Core visual idea
**Surface vs. underlying compliance layer.**

The site avoids generic shields, legal symbols, and decorative AI imagery. The hero shows the same website twice: a normal surface UI in front, and a transparent line-drawing version behind it where a few gaps become visible. That visual language continues through the rest of the page as reveal, missing piece, status, and escalation metaphors.

## Information hierarchy
1. Hero establishes the product and primary action without scrolling.
2. How it works sets the entire journey before breaking it into Analyze / Improve / Monitor.
3. Verified Coverage translates technical findings into plain-language business meaning.
4. +Human introduces lawyer supervision only after the core AI product is understood.
5. Pricing plans are visually equal, per the assignment rules.
6. Trust and FAQ remove remaining objections.
7. Final CTA repeats Analyze your website and accurately sets the signup → conversation expectation.

## Accessibility considerations
- Semantic headings and landmarks.
- Keyboard-focus styles.
- Native `<details>` FAQ.
- Accessible URL labels.
- Reduced-motion support.
- Body copy and interactive labels sized for readability.
- Status colors always paired with text or symbols.
